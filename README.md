# GeziGo-privacy-policy

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

Gezigo operates the Gezigo mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use
Gezigo is designed to be used without any user registration or account creation. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Location Data
We use your precise location data to provide and improve our services, specifically for:

Identifying and displaying nearby places, venues, and points of interest.

Providing accurate directions and navigation within the app.

This data is processed to fulfill your requests in real-time. Since there is no account system, this location data is not linked to your identity and is not stored on our servers in a way that identifies you.

3. Log Data and Identifiers
We may collect information that your browser or device sends whenever you use our Service ("Log Data"). This Log Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device version, and app performance statistics. We use unique device identifiers only for app functionality and internal analytics to improve user experience.

4. Third-Party Services
Our app utilizes standard map services (such as Apple Maps) to provide navigation. These third-party services have their own Privacy Policies regarding how they handle location data. We do not share your location with any third-party advertising networks.

5. Tracking and Cookies
We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies to track your activity across other apps or websites for advertising purposes.
